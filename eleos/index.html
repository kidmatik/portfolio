One session at a time.

Vision, mission and values

Everyone gets the care they deserve.

Vision

At Eleos, our vision is simple: everyone gets the behavioral healthcare they deserve.

Mission

We’re on a mission to help more people with mental illness get well sooner by optimizing the quality and effectiveness of the behavioral healthcare they receive.

By doing this, we’ll play our part in solving the public behavioral health crisis by enabling access to quality behavioral healthcare, at a scale not currently possible.

Core values

  • Each behavioral health session is a precious opportunity to bring individuals a step closer to living a happier, healthier and more fulfilled and productive life.
  • We believe struggling with mental health-related issues deserve the best care possible.
  • We believe technology and objective data can empower clinicians and their patients.
The logo

Three rounded bars build the letter E.

The logo represents the letter E, timeline and conversation elements from the Eleos product, and visualizes progress and motion. The middle bar steps to the left, and the symbol sits on a two-unit modular grid.

Do’s and don’ts

One logo, always consistent.

The logo should not be misinterpreted, modified or altered. Its orientation, colour and composition stay exactly as defined, with no exceptions.

  • Never use the old version of the logo
  • Never distort the proportions or change elements relative to each other
  • Never add contours or change its colours
  • Minimum size: 160px in digital, 45mm in print
