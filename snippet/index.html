Product design · Snippet Finance

Financing on fairer terms.

The corporate website and the loan application platform for Snippet Finance, designed for desktop and mobile, in English and Japanese.

Client
Snippet Finance
Role
Senior Brand & Product Designer
Scope
Website, loan application platform
The mission

Design the platform that lends fairly.

Design the loan application platform and the front-end website for Snippet Finance. Snippet helps employees access financing on fairer terms.

By deducting a small amount (a “snippet”) from salary each month towards loan repayment, Snippet provides an affordable way to consolidate debt or top up finances when needed. Here is some of the work.

Corporate websiteLoan application platformDesktop & mobileEnglish & Japanese
The system

One site, one platform, every screen.

The marketing pages, the employee journey and the application flow share one clean visual language, from the laptop down to the phone.

The application

How much would you like to borrow?

The application is a short, guided flow of four steps. Each screen asks one thing, shows progress at the top and keeps the amount, the term and the next action in view, on a laptop or on a phone.

Step 1 of 4, Loan info, on desktop and mobile.

Two regions

One entry, two sites.

The first screen explains the idea in a few lines, then lets visitors pick their region, the US site or the Japanese one, so the same product speaks both languages.

The Japanese version

Same flow, localised for Japan.

The Japanese site keeps the structure and the guided steps, and adapts the details: yen amounts, Japanese labels, a start-date field and a co-branded header with 横浜銀行 (Bank of Yokohama) under the FuturePay name.
