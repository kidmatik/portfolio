Brand, website & product UI/UX case study · Trio

Cloud, the next generation.

A new logo, brand identity, website and product UI/UX for Trio Cloud Technologies, an Israeli cloud and cyber company that gives resellers servers, backup, managed cyber protection and AI infrastructure.

Role
Senior Brand & Product Designer
Scope
Logo, brand, website, product UI/UX
Industry
Cloud and cyber security
Year

The company

25 years in the cloud, ready for what’s next.

Trio has provided cloud computing services since 2000, with two ISO standards in information security and business continuity.

Its customers are resellers and technology-heavy companies. Trio runs their servers, backup and cyber protection, and the new brand and website had to say it with the same confidence the service delivers.

  • Backup and business continuity
  • Managed cyber service

The logo

A cloud, wrapped in a loop.

The symbol joins a cloud with a circular arrow that runs through it, the motion of data syncing, backing up and recovering. The wordmark is set in clean, rounded capitals, and the colour moves from a warm orange to amber, like a sunrise over the cloud.
