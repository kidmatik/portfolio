Brand case study · BK Digital

Designing the brand behind the product. Full branding process.

The logo, visual language, marketing materials and print for BK Digital, a cloud-based, AI-powered system that automates accounting and financial management.

Role
Senior Brand & Product Designer
Scope
Full branding process
Industry
AI accounting SaaS
Deliverables
Logo, visual language, marketing, print
The mission

Design the brand behind the product.

From logo to visual language, marketing materials and print design, the brand had to explain a new Paperless Accounting Automated System.

BK Digital is a cloud-based, AI-powered system that automates all aspects of accounting and financial management in organizations. The brand needed to feel as clear and dependable as the product.

Logo exploration

Finding the right mark.

I explored several lockups for the wordmark, from a horizontal logotype to a compact badge, before settling on the version that reads well at every size.

The process

A paperless workflow, explained in six steps.

The brand carried a simple story: invoices move through the system from capture to management, with no paper in between.

  1. CaptureDocuments are scanned or uploaded
  2. RecognizeThe system reads the data
  3. CollaborateTeams review and comment together
  4. ApproveThe right person signs off
  5. Post to accountingRecords go into the accounting system
  6. ManageEverything stays organized and traceable

The brand on the web: bold type, a single orange line and plenty of white space.

Wrap-up

A brand that makes accounting feel simple.

The brand connects the product from its first screen to its print materials, so BK Digital speaks with one clear voice.

A special thanks to the brilliant and friendly people at Brooks-Keret.
